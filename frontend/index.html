For families who still pick up the phone

Keep the scammer talking, not your parents.

Scammer Ko Scam Kar listens to a call as it happens, recognises fake-police, digital-arrest, KYC and courier scripts, and lets an AI decoy — a confused, chatty retired schoolteacher — stall the caller while it quietly writes down their UPI ID, account number and phone number.

Runs on your own machine; transcripts stay in a local file, nothing is uploaded elsewhere. If money has already been sent, call the national cyber-crime helpline 1930 before anything else.
